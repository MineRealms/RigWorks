## Ownership

TaCZ owns first-person weapon animation and camera transforms. RigWorks replaces
the arm geometry at the hand draw boundary. Its third-person character runtime
consumes TaCZ state and discrete action generations. YSM assets use their authored
gun clips and controllers; other humanoids can consume the native humanoid pose.

Native item dimensions are independent of an avatar's source units. Locator
positions stay in the avatar's space, while authored equipment offsets are metres.
No YSM decryption, reflection discovery, or geometry selection belongs in a draw
loop. Optional TaCZ entry points are cached; state belongs to individual players.
